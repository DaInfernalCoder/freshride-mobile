Beyond the flat rate

When a vehicle needs more than the package.

01

Vehicle size

The flat rates cover most cars, crossovers, and SUVs. Three-row SUVs and work trucks may carry a surcharge, quoted before we book.

02

Heavy condition

Months of hard buildup can run past the package. Send two or three photos and we will confirm any extra before the visit, never on arrival day.

03

Problem areas

Heavy pet hair, set-in stains, odors, sap, and bonded contamination need extra treatment. We price those on top of the package and tell you first.

04

Extra finish work

Protection and paint-enhancement options sit outside the packages above and are quoted separately if you want them.
