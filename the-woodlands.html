Detailing in The Woodlands

What The Woodlands does to a car.

The Woodlands is built under a pine canopy, and that canopy is hard on paint. Pine sap drips onto roofs and hoods and hardens into spots that a normal wash slides right past. Needles collect in cowls and door channels. Shaded driveways stay damp, and birds in mature trees do the rest. It's the most tree-driven detailing market in the metro, and it needs a different approach than an open suburban driveway.

Good to know

The Woodlands questions, answered.

Anything else, bring it to the call and we'll make sure you know exactly what to expect.

Can you get pine sap off my roof and hood?+

Yes, that's the number one thing we're called for in The Woodlands. Fresh sap lifts cleanly. Sap that's been hardening for months may have etched the clear coat underneath, and we'll tell you what we see before we start.

Do you come out to Creekside Park and Sterling Ridge?+

Yes, along with Alden Bridge, Grogan's Mill, Panther Creek and the rest of the villages.

My driveway is fully shaded. Is that a problem?+

It's the opposite of a problem. Shade means the panels stay cool and products don't flash off before we can work them. Shaded driveways are the best case.
