Local conditions

What Thompsons does to your car

Thompsons' rural setting means agricultural dust and field runoff affect paint during plowing and harvest seasons. Gulf humidity remains constant despite the rural location. Tree pollen (oak in spring, ragweed in fall) settles on exposed vehicles. Hard water from agricultural wells creates spotting. Lovebugs peak in August-September. Before you book

Thompsons car detailing questions.

Does farm dust really damage my car?+

Yes. Agricultural dust is finer and sticks harder when mixed with humidity. It accumulates faster in rural areas where wind is slower. Regular detailing prevents buildup from becoming permanent oxidation and scratches.

Why is hard water from wells a problem?+

Agricultural well water has high mineral content. When it dries on paint or windows, it leaves white spotting and film. Detailing removes the mineral deposits; ceramic coating prevents them from sticking.

Is Thompsons far from detailers?+

Yes—the nearest shops are in Richmond or Rosenberg, 12+ miles away. Mobile detailing is practical for Thompsons because we come to your driveway instead of you making a long drive.
