Sienna car detailing questions.

Why is water spotting such a big problem in Sienna?+

Sienna's 3,000+ acres of parks, golf courses, and lakes generate mineral-laden irrigation. The Brazos River and Oyster Creek nearby amplify humidity. Sprinkler spray and morning dew dry on paint, leaving mineral deposits that etch clear coat. Ceramic coating prevents mineral adhesion, while monthly waxing reduces water-spot visibility.

Can new cars in Sienna develop mold?+

Yes. Sienna's persistent humidity and water features promote mold growth in air-conditioning systems and upholstery faster than in drier areas. Regular interior detailing with antimicrobial treatments prevents odors and mold. Ensure your car's air filter is clean, and use air-conditioning regularly to prevent moisture buildup.

Should I ceramic-coat my new Sienna vehicle?+

Highly recommended. Ceramic coating protects new paint from Sienna's mineral-spotting and construction-dust environment. Applied early, it helps keep your new vehicle looking fresh. The hydrophobic properties are especially valuable given the community's water-intensive landscape.
