Stafford car detailing questions.

Does Stafford's construction dust damage my paint?+

Construction dust is abrasive and can scratch clear coat if rubbed with a dry cloth. Regular professional detailing removes dust safely before it embeds, preventing swirl marks and long-term oxidation.

How often should I detail my car in Stafford?+

Every 3–4 months is typical, depending on parking location. Vehicles near highways or commercial areas benefit from quarterly service to manage dust, pollen, and hard-water exposure.
