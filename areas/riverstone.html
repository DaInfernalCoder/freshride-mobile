Local conditions

What Riverstone does to your car

Riverstone's water features and extensive tree canopy keep humidity persistently high, promoting corrosion and mold growth on vehicles. The 200-acre lake system and 3-acre recreation complex means morning dew and sprinkler moisture linger on paint longer than in drier areas. Pollen from landscaping and nearby prairies settles heavily during spring and fall. Highway 6 traffic deposits fine dust and brake residue. The community's ongoing development in its final phases brings construction activity and dust. Summer heat reaches 94 degrees, baking interiors.
