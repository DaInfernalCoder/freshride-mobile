Local conditions

What Fresno does to your car

Fresno's location on FM 521 and Highway 6 means constant exposure to commute dust and heavy traffic from drivers accessing Highway 6 toward Pearland and Houston. Active development brings construction dust to the area. Proximity to Missouri City and Houston's sprawl means continued building activity and disruption. Gulf humidity (75%+) traps dust on paint longer. Spring oak pollen (February-May) and fall ragweed (August-November) are heavy. Lovebugs swarm August-September. Newly built neighborhoods mean irrigation systems add water spotting from hard water. Road construction and ongoing commercial development create variable air quality.
