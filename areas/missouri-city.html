Before you book

Missouri City car detailing questions.

Why does tree sap damage car paint?+

Tree sap is acidic and hardens quickly in heat. If left, it etches through clear coat into base paint. Regular removal prevents permanent damage. Once etched, only paint correction restores the finish.

How do I prevent hard-water spotting in Missouri City?+

Park in shade when possible and remove water spots weekly. Professional waxing and ceramic coating repel water, minimizing spotting. Regular detailing catches spots before they etch into the finish.

Is Quail Valley's golf course water bad for my car?+

Golf course irrigation water is typically untreated and leaves heavy mineral deposits. Vehicles parked nearby see accelerated water spotting. Regular detailing and ceramic coating are particularly valuable for these properties.
