Describe the material and condition first. Loose debris and routine cabin surfaces differ from a fabric-treatment request. Confirm the included checklist and any additional treatment.

Can the car be used immediately after upholstery cleaning?+

Ask before booking. The treatment, material, weather, and ventilation affect readiness. Explain when the car is needed so drying can be part of the plan.
