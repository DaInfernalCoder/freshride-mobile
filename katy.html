Detailing in Katy

What Katy does to a car.

Katy has two things working against clean paint. The first is construction — the area is still being built, and fine dust from active sites settles on everything parked outside. The second is sprinkler water. Katy's water is hard, and lawn sprinklers throwing it onto a car door leave mineral spots that a regular wash will not lift once they've sat in the sun.

Good to know

Katy questions, answered.

Anything else, bring it to the call and we'll make sure you know exactly what to expect.

Do you come out to Cinco Ranch and Cross Creek Ranch?+

Yes, both, along with Grand Lakes, Seven Meadows, Firethorne and the rest of Katy. Gated sections just need gate access sorted before we arrive.

Can you remove the hard water spots from my sprinklers?+

In most cases, yes. Sprinkler mineral spotting is one of the most common things we're called for in Katy. If it's been baking for months it can etch the clear coat, so mention it on the call and we'll set expectations honestly.

My HOA has rules about washing in the driveway. Is that a problem?+

Tell us the rule and we'll work with it. We bring our own setup and we're not looking to get you a letter from the HOA over a clean car.
