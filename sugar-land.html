Detailing in Sugar Land

What Sugar Land does to a car.

Sugar Land is manicured, and the irrigation that keeps it that way is what marks up the cars. Hard water from sprinklers spots doors and lower panels. The mature tree cover through First Colony and Sugar Creek adds the other half: sap, pollen, and bird droppings that etch a clear coat if they sit through a hot week.

Good to know

Sugar Land questions, answered.

Anything else, bring it to the call and we'll make sure you know exactly what to expect.

Do you service gated communities like Riverstone and Telfair?+

Yes. Gated is normal for Sugar Land. Just add us at the gate or meet us there — we can't get in without it.

Can you get tree sap off without damaging the paint?+

Usually. Fresh sap comes off cleanly. Sap that's sat through a Texas summer can etch into the clear coat, and at that point it's a paint correction question rather than a wash question. We'll look and tell you which one you're dealing with.

Do you detail at the office parks around Town Square?+

Yes, if the lot allows it. Plenty of Sugar Land customers have us come to the office instead of the house. We confirm the lot is fine with it first.
