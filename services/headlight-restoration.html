Headlight Restoration in Sugar Land, TX

Plastic headlight lenses cloud and yellow as the factory UV coating wears away under Texas sun, which dims the beam and makes the whole front end look tired. Sugar Land Car Detailing restores foggy, hazy and yellowed headlights by sanding away the oxidized layer, polishing the lens clear and sealing it with UV protection. We come to a suitable location you arrange, and we confirm what is realistic for each lens before work begins.

What is included in headlight restoration.

  • Masking of surrounding paint and trim
  • Staged wet sanding to remove the oxidized outer layer
  • Polishing to restore clarity
  • UV-protective sealant over the finished lens

Suitability and limits

What restoration can and cannot fix

Restoration treats the outside of the lens. It cannot fix cracked lenses, moisture or condensation trapped inside the housing, or a failing bulb or reflector. Heavily damaged lenses may improve but not return to like-new, and any restored lens will yellow again over time if it is left unprotected, which is why the UV sealing step matters. Replacement may be the better answer for deep pitting, cracks or internal damage.

Customer questions

Headlight Restoration: questions before you book.

Is headlight restoration worth it?+

For oxidized or yellowed lenses it can make a big difference to appearance and night visibility at a fraction of replacement cost. Cracked or moisture-filled lenses need replacement.

How long do restored headlights stay clear?+

It varies with sun exposure, parking and washing. A UV-protective sealant slows re-yellowing, but we do not promise a fixed duration.

Can you fix headlights with moisture inside?+

No. Condensation or water inside the housing is a seal problem. Restoration only works on the outer lens surface.

Do you restore both headlights?+

Yes. Both lenses are restored so they match. Tell us if only one is cloudy and we will quote accordingly.
