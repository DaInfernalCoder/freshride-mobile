A complete reset, inside and out.

Fresh Ride's Full Reset is the most complete starting point for a vehicle that needs attention inside and out. We bring mobile detailing to a suitable driveway, workplace, or approved parking location across Greater Houston, then work through the exterior, wheels, tires, cabin, surfaces, and glass. Because vehicle size and condition change the amount of work, we confirm the scope and quote before the visit rather than publishing a price that may not fit your car. That up-front review keeps the visit focused and lets us bring the right setup for the work you actually want at your location.

Good to know

Full Reset questions.

Clear answers before we pull up.

What is included in a Full Reset?+

The Full Reset combines the core exterior wash, wheels and tires, exterior glass, interior vacuum, cabin surfaces, trim, and interior glass. We confirm the exact checklist and any optional treatment before the appointment.

How much does a Full Reset cost?+

The quote depends on vehicle size, condition, and requested add-ons. Call or book a time to describe the vehicle, and we will confirm the scope and price before the visit.

Can you handle pet hair, stains, or odors?+

In many cases, yes. These can change the time and treatment required, so mention every problem area when you call. We will set expectations before booking.

Where can you perform the detail?+

At a suitable driveway, workplace, or other approved parking location across Greater Houston. We confirm parking and any site-specific access needs before arrival.
