Good to know

Exterior Refresh questions.

Clear answers before we pull up.

What is included in the Exterior Refresh?+

The confirmed service includes a careful exterior wash and dry, wheels and tires, exterior glass, and the selected finishing protection. We review the vehicle condition before work begins.

Can a wash remove scratches or etched water spots?+

Not always. Some defects sit in or below the paint surface and may require paint enhancement or correction. Describe the issue on the call and we will separate wash work from correction work honestly.

What happens if the weather changes?+

If weather makes the work unsafe or would compromise the finish, we coordinate a better time rather than hand back a result we are not comfortable with.

Do you service trucks and SUVs?+

Yes. Vehicle size affects the scope and quote, so tell us the year, make, model, and condition when you call or book online.

Is interior cleaning included in Exterior Refresh?+

The stated Exterior Refresh scope covers the outside of the vehicle. Full Reset combines the core interior and exterior work; choose the service that matches the areas needing attention and confirm the checklist before booking.

What should I mention about existing paint or surface damage?+

Describe scratches, sap, mineral spots, damaged trim, and any film, wrap, or recent paintwork before booking. These details help establish whether the planned cleaning and finishing step are suitable or whether a separate treatment needs discussion.
