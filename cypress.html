What Cypress does to a car.

Cypress is still going up, and it shows on the cars. Bridgeland and the newer Grand Parkway sections have active construction throwing fine dust that settles into a gritty film — the kind that scratches if you wipe it dry. The lakes at Towne Lake and Bridgeland bring the other half: bugs on the front end and a pollen load in spring that a quick rinse doesn't touch.

Good to know

Cypress questions, answered.

Anything else, bring it to the call and we'll make sure you know exactly what to expect.

Do you detail in Bridgeland and Towne Lake?+

Yes, plus Fairfield, Coles Crossing, Blackhorse Ranch and the rest of Cypress.

My SUV interior is destroyed — kids, dogs, sports gear. Can you handle it?+

That's most of our Cypress work. Mention pet hair, spills, and problem spots when you call so we plan the right amount of time. Heavy interiors take longer and we'd rather quote that honestly up front.

There's construction dust all over my car constantly. Is detailing even worth it?+

Fair question. A detail resets it, but if you're parked next to an active site it will come back. A protective finish makes the next dust layer rinse off instead of grinding in, and that's the part that actually helps between details.
